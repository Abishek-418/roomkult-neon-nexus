# ROOMKULT Neon Nexus

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and front-end web developer. I am building an e-commerce website for my room decor brand, ROOMKULT. We sell edgy, alternative posters and interior decor (mirrors, rugs) targeted heavily at GenZ (ages 15-25).

Design Aesthetic & Vibe: The brand name contains "KULT," and the website must reflect an underground, edgy, and highly atmospheric vibe. The color palette is strictly deep black and toxic/neon green. The visual theme should feel slightly surreal or cyber-goth, reminiscent of night-vision aesthetics, while remaining highly functional for e-commerce.

Typography & Motion:

Typography: Use clean, modern, and sharp sans-serif typography. The text must be highly legible and contrast well against the dark backgrounds to keep the site looking premium.

Animations: Keep motion and background effects minimal on the Home and Gallery pages to ensure the products stand out as the absolute focal point. You may use subtle, atmospheric effects (like a very faint glow or slight glitch effect on hover) sparingly on secondary elements or the Contact page to maintain the vibe without distracting the buyer.

Scope of Work (Phase 1): Please generate the front-end layout and UI for Phase 1, which must include the following:

Home/Landing Page: A high-impact hero section that immediately establishes the "kult" vibe. It should prominently feature our green/black logo and have a strong call-to-action to shop the collection. Use dark mode UI elements.

Poster Gallery/Shop Interface: A visually engaging, clean grid gallery to showcase our poster designs. Please include filtering/category options for the following genres: Cars, Sports, Movies, Games, and More. The products themselves must be the primary focus of this page.

Contact Us Page: A stylized, easy-to-use contact form that fits the dark, underground theme.

Note on Future Development: This is strictly Phase 1. Focus entirely on the aesthetic perfection, layout, and user experience of these specific pages. Phase 2 details (such as advanced cart integrations, user accounts, and additional product categories) will be provided only after Phase 1 is completed and perfected.
